# Deepgram API 2026: Nova-3 STT & Aura-2 TTS — Voice AI Powerhouse

## Introduction

Deepgram was founded in 2015 with a mission to make voice data accessible and actionable through AI. Unlike many voice API providers that started as consumer products and added APIs later, Deepgram was built API-first from day one. Their core differentiator is end-to-end deep learning: instead of stitching together traditional speech recognition pipelines (acoustic model + language model + decoder), Deepgram trains a single neural network end-to-end on raw audio.

## What Deepgram Actually Offers

### Speech-to-Text (STT) — Nova-3 & Flux

Deepgram's flagship product is its STT API. Three model families cover different use cases: Nova-3 Monolingual (best accuracy English), Nova-3 Multilingual (50+ languages), Flux (conversational with turn detection), and Whisper (open model).

Pricing: STT from $0.0043/min (Nova-3 streaming) to $0.0077/min (Flux pre-recorded).

### Text-to-Speech (TTS) — Aura-2 & Aura-1

Deepgram's TTS offering, branded as Aura, provides natural-sounding speech synthesis. Aura-2 offers expressive, natural speech at $0.030/1K chars; Aura-1 offers high quality at $0.015/1K chars. Sub-200ms latency.

### Voice Agent API

A newer product combining STT + TTS + LLM into a unified voice agent pipeline.

## Pricing: Pay-as-You-Go with $200 Free Credits

Deepgram uses straightforward pay-as-you-go pricing. Growth plan (~$1,000+/month) offers 12-20% discount. Startup program with up to $100K in credits.

## Pros and Cons

Pros: Best-in-class STT accuracy (54% WER reduction), sub-300ms latency, $200 free credits, 50+ STT languages, developer-friendly, self-hosted option, SOC 2/HIPAA/GDPR compliant.

Cons: TTS only 7 languages (no Chinese), no China data center, no affiliate program, add-on features cost extra.

## FAQ

Q: How accurate is Nova-3 for English STT?
A: Best-in-class WER with 54% reduction over Nova-2. Clean audio ~3-5% WER, conversational ~8-12%, noisy ~12-18%.

Q: Does Deepgram support Chinese?
A: Yes for STT (Mandarin, Cantonese via Nova-3 Multilingual/Flux). No for TTS.

Q: Can I use Deepgram from China?
A: Yes with proxy. No mainland China data center.

## Conclusion

Deepgram is the go-to choice for STT accuracy and latency. Nova-3 is the most accurate English speech recognition API at a competitive price. Self-hosted option is a standout for enterprise.
